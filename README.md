# 🥋 The Karate Kid (2010)

> *"Life will knock us down, but we can choose whether or not to stand back up."* — **Mr. Han**

## 📌 Overview
**The Karate Kid** (2010) is a martial arts drama directed by *Harald Zwart*. It follows 12-year-old **Dre Parker** as he moves from Detroit to Beijing, China, and learns kung fu from his maintenance man, **Mr. Han**, to defend himself against bullies and compete in an open kung fu tournament.

## 🌟 Main Characters
* **Dre Parker** (*Jaden Smith*) – A young boy struggling to adapt to a new culture who finds confidence and discipline through martial arts.
* **Mr. Han** (*Jackie Chan*) – A quiet, skilled kung fu master dealing with past grief who mentors Dre using everyday routines.
* **Cheng** (*Zhenwei Wang*) – Dre's main rival and top student at the ruthless Fighting Dragon studio.

---

## 🔥 Memorable Training Moments
1. **Jacket On, Jacket Off:** Mr. Han teaches Dre basic defensive blocks and muscle memory through repetitive jacket drills.
2. **Wudang Mountains:** Dre visits the birthplace of kung fu and witnesses true balance and focus.
3. **The Final Tournament:** Dre overcomes a leg injury to defeat Cheng using a crane-inspired kick.
